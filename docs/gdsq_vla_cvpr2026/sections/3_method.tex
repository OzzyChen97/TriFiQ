\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Overview.}
\method converts a pretrained VLA policy into a frozen mixed-precision policy and a model-level correction decision without updating model parameters. The allocation pipeline in Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline} has four stages: isolated layer interventions, dual-similarity and action scoring, constrained mask search, and paired functional adjudication. A fifth stage calibrates ATM and OHB for the selected mask and applies a preregistered absolute gate. The deployed output is therefore a pair $(M,v)$, where $M$ is a W4/FP16 mask and $v\in\{\text{baseline},\text{ATM},\text{OHB}\}$ is fixed for the model.

\subsection{Isolated Quantization Interventions}
\label{sec:setting}

Given visual observations $I$, instruction $l$, robot state $s$, and denoising time $t$, a VLA policy iteratively updates an action sample
\begin{equation}
    x_{t-1}=f_{\theta}(x_t,F_{\mathrm{VL}}(I,l),s,t).
    \label{eq:vla}
\end{equation}
We use group-wise DuQuant-style W4 weights and static A8 activations with group size 64~\cite{lin2024duquant}. The selectable inventory is checkpoint-specific. GR00T contains 116 candidate Linear layers---84 language projections and 32 DiT feed-forward projections---while 64 protected DiT attention projections remain FP16. The $\pi_{0.5}$ inventory contains 180 language and action-expert Linear layers.

A single reference path cannot separate W4 attribution from wrapper mechanics. We therefore use an \emph{attribution reference} $R$ that retains the transformations and A8 wrappers but routes every candidate weight through its full-precision branch. The \emph{deployment reference} is the unwrapped FP16 policy. For candidate $i$, all layers follow $R$ except $i$, which uses W4. A fixed calibration buffer, token mask, and initial action noise are shared between the intervention and $R$. Hooks collect matched outputs $(H_i^R,H_i^Q)$; padding rows are removed with the reference mask, and deterministic stratified pooling prevents visual, language, or state tokens from dominating. Representation and single-layer action scores use $R$, whereas activation guards and complete-mask adjudication use the deployment reference.

\subsection{Dual Similarity, Action Weighting, and Guards}
\label{sec:dual_scores}

CKA measures preservation of inter-sample geometry. For centered matrices $\widetilde H_i^R,\widetilde H_i^Q\in\mathbb{R}^{N\times d}$, let $G_i^{UV}=(\widetilde H_i^U)^{\!\top}\widetilde H_i^V$. We compute
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{CKA}_i=
\frac{\|G_i^{RQ}\|_F^2}
{\|G_i^{RR}\|_F\|G_i^{QQ}\|_F+\epsilon},
\qquad \dcka_i=1-\operatorname{CKA}_i.
\label{eq:cka}
\end{equation}
Its invariance to isotropic scaling is useful for stable geometry comparison but creates a scale blind spot. We complement it with an empirical CS divergence. For Gaussian kernel $k$ whose bandwidth is fixed from the reference samples,
\begin{align}
\dcs_i={}&\log\!\left(\frac{1}{N^2}\sum_{a,b}k(h_a^R,h_b^R)\right)
+\log\!\left(\frac{1}{N^2}\sum_{a,b}k(h_a^Q,h_b^Q)\right)\nonumber\\
&-2\log\!\left(\frac{1}{N^2}\sum_{a,b}k(h_a^R,h_b^Q)\right).
\label{eq:cs}
\end{align}
We use log-sum-exp accumulation and a bandwidth floor of $10^{-3}$. After joint min--max normalization across candidates, the representation score is
\begin{equation}
S_i=\lambda_{\mathrm{CKA}}\overline{\dcka}_i+
    \lambda_{\mathrm{CS}}\overline{\dcs}_i.
\label{eq:dual_score}
\end{equation}

Representation damage matters only when it changes control. Under the same isolated intervention and paired denoising noise, let $d_i$ denote action-trajectory divergence from $R$. We define
\begin{equation}
    w_i=\frac{n d_i}{\sum_j d_j+\epsilon},
    \label{eq:action_weight}
\end{equation}
then robustly clip and renormalize the weights so that $n^{-1}\sum_i w_i=1$. This distinguishes layers with similar representation distortion but different downstream effects.

Similarity is not a stability certificate. Each intervention also records median log-RMS drift from unwrapped FP16 and the estimated fraction beyond the downstream static-A8 range. GR00T uses the frozen thresholds $\tau_{\mathrm{RMS}}=0.1319$ and $\tau_{\mathrm{sat}}=0.00232$; $\pi_{0.5}$ uses its separately calibrated thresholds. A violation forces FP16. The saturation statistic is an engineering approximation based on the calibrated consumer scale, not a direct measurement of fused-kernel clipping.

\subsection{Byte-Constrained Search and Functional Adjudication}
\label{sec:selection}

Let $x_i=1$ assign W4A8 and $x_i=0$ preserve the original FP16 Linear. Candidate masks minimize
\begin{align}
\min_x\quad &\sum_i x_i w_i S_i, \label{eq:objective}\\
\text{s.t.}\quad
&\sum_i\left[x_i C_i^{\mathrm{W4}}+(1-x_i)C_i^{\mathrm{FP16}}\right]\le B,\nonumber\\
&x_i=0\quad\text{for every guard violation},\qquad x_i\in\{0,1\}.\nonumber
\end{align}
Here $C_i$ is theoretical tightly packed weight storage. The uniform-W6 candidate-scope budgets are $862{,}912{,}512$ bytes for GR00T and $2{,}042{,}542{,}080$ bytes for $\pi_{0.5}$. They exclude activations, CUDA workspaces, simulator state, and fake-quant caches. Greedy allocation and local bit flips produce diverse low-objective masks; the additive optimum is not treated as the final policy.

Simultaneous interventions can cancel or amplify. We therefore instantiate selected Top-$K$ masks with plan-specific A8 calibration and compare complete policies under paired observations and noise. The functional score is
\begin{equation}
\dfunc=D_{\mathrm{final}}+D_{\mathrm{kin}}+D_{\mathrm{grip}}
       +2\operatorname{CVaR}_{0.9}(D_{\mathrm{solver}}),
\label{eq:dfunc}
\end{equation}
where the tail term prevents a low mean from hiding severe denoising failures. Functional adjudication selects a representative for each CKA:CS ratio. On four predeclared GR00T development tasks, the frozen rule selects 16:1 from $\{8,16,32,64,128,256\}:1$ and a CKA-only boundary. The final masks assign W4 to 100 of 116 GR00T candidates and 80 of 180 $\pi_{0.5}$ candidates. A 10,000-draw task/seed bootstrap and leave-one-task-out analysis conducted after freezing finds no ratio whose paired interval against 16:1 excludes zero; we therefore interpret 16:1 as a selected point on a broad development plateau, not a universal optimum.

\subsection{Calibration-Gated Runtime Correction}
\label{sec:runtime_selector}

The mixed-precision mask can leave structured attention drift, but a correction can also destabilize an already adequate policy. For the frozen mask, ATM calibrates a query-temperature multiplier $\alpha$ and OHB calibrates per-head output multipliers $\beta_h$. We first choose one \emph{candidate} mechanism from their model-level calibration geometry. OHB is the candidate when its correction is broad and its mean absolute log-magnitude is at least twice that of ATM. ATM is the candidate when corrected attention projections remain FP16 and its directional coherence exceeds OHB by at least 0.05. Otherwise the candidate is the baseline. ATM and OHB are not composed because their ratios act at different points of the same propagated attention error and are not calibrated as an additive correction.

The v8 absolute gate then decides whether to admit the candidate. For correction values $c_1,\ldots,c_m$, define
\begin{align}
q_{\mathrm{dir}}&=\frac{|n_{<1}-n_{>1}|}{m}, &
q_{\mathrm{non}}&=\frac{n_{c\ne1}}{m},\nonumber\\
q_{\log}&=\frac{1}{m}\sum_j|\log c_j|.&&
\label{eq:selector_gate}
\end{align}
The correction is enabled only if $q_{\mathrm{dir}}\ge0.30$, $q_{\mathrm{non}}\ge0.85$, and $q_{\log}\ge0.05$; otherwise the baseline is selected. These three thresholds and the selector SHA were frozen before formal testing. GR00T's ATM candidate fails all three gates and therefore selects the uncorrected GDSQ mask. The $\pi_{0.5}$ OHB candidate passes all three and selects OHB. The decision is evaluated once per model, so task names retained in runtime metadata are attestations only and cannot affect the selected variant.

Every formal result row records the selector SHA, rule name, selected variant, selected configuration ID, and server-metadata hash. For GR00T, whose bitwise gate permits reuse of the immutable static matrix, a separate 2,500-row derived attestation adds these selector fields and the source-row hash without modifying the original outcomes. The runtime rejects an unknown variant and v8 rejects ATM+OHB. Consequently, the final method is exactly GDSQ-VLA plus one frozen model-level choice from $\{\text{baseline},\text{ATM},\text{OHB}\}$; static GDSQ, ATM, OHB, and ATM+OHB configurations are ablations.
