\section{Conclusion and Limitations}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presents \method, a training-free framework that couples action-weighted geometry/distribution preservation with byte-constrained W4/FP16 allocation and a calibration-gated runtime correction selector. The allocation uses CKA, CS divergence, stability guards, and complete-mask functional adjudication; the selector makes one frozen model-level choice among baseline, ATM, and OHB without task identity or outcome feedback. Bitwise path tests pass on 256 frozen observations for both policy families. The completed GR00T selector obtains 50.8\% task-macro SR over 50 RoboCasa365 tasks at a theoretical 1.99$\times$ candidate-component compression, compared with 55.1\% for FP16. \PiSelectorResultStatus

The evidence also defines clear boundaries. The large GR00T and $\pi_{0.5}$ differences from QuantVLA are secondary, different-compression comparisons. \SameBudgetConclusionStatus\ The 16:1 ratio lies on a broad four-task development plateau and is transferred, rather than independently selected by $\pi_{0.5}$ rollouts. Finally, the present fake-quant path demonstrates theoretical static storage only: it does not reduce live GPU memory or latency.

Future work will recalibrate and evaluate the method on all four LIBERO suites, measure mask and selector stability across calibration sizes and prompts, trace a frozen-budget storage--SR Pareto frontier, and report packed checkpoint size, isolated policy-server memory, and batch-1 latency. Shared-protocol comparisons with ActQuant, HoloQ-VLA, and SQAP-VLA require reproducible official implementations; otherwise, the preregistered action-only control remains the appropriate internal baseline. Fused INT4/INT8 kernels and real-robot evaluation are necessary before making an end-to-end deployment claim.
