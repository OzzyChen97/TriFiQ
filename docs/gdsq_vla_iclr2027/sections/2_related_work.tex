\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Efficient VLA deployment.}
Generalist VLA policies combine multimodal encoders with continuous-control heads~\cite{brohan2023rt2,octo2024,kim2024openvla,black2024pi0,intelligence2025pi05,bjorck2025groot}. Compact architectures, token reduction, temporal reuse, and architecture--quantizer co-design can reduce computation as well as storage~\cite{shukor2025smolvla,zhang2025molevla,yang2025efficientvla,xu2025vlacache,fang2025sqapvla}. We retain the pretrained architecture and study its success--storage tradeoff. Runtime efficiency is a separate empirical question: Appendix~\ref{sec:hardware_results} reports both the memory benefit and latency cost of the evaluated backend.

\paragraph{Action-aware VLA quantization.}
QuantVLA adapts distribution transformations to language and diffusion-action stacks~\cite{zhang2026quantvla}; ActQuant allocates tensor precision using action contribution and action-aware curvature~\cite{akbari2026actquant}; $\Omega$-QVLA combines rotations with denoising-step scaling~\cite{wang2026omegaqvla}; and DA-PTQ uses trajectory motion drift for mixed-precision VLA quantization~\cite{xu2026daptq}. These approaches already move beyond generic tensor reconstruction, so action awareness alone is not our distinguishing claim. We couple complete-mask deviation auditing under exact byte constraints with input-conditioned activation ranges, and use controlled rollouts to examine where these choices help. Local architecture adaptations of ActQuant and DA-PTQ are explicitly separated from their source protocols.

\paragraph{Calibration and dynamic activation ranges.}
GPTQ and AWQ use second-order information or activation salience for weight quantization~\cite{frantar2022gptq,lin2024awq}. Weight--activation PTQ uses smoothing, calibration, or rotations~\cite{xiao2023smoothquant,shao2024omniquant,ashkboos2024quarot,lin2024duquant,liu2025spinquant,sun2025flatquant}, while diffusion PTQ addresses timestep-dependent ranges and iterative error accumulation~\cite{shang2023ptq4dm,li2023qdiffusion,wu2024ptq4dit,chen2024qdit,zhao2024mixdq}. Input-dependent scaling is established in ZeroQuant~\cite{yao2022zeroquant} and ViDiT-Q~\cite{zhao2024viditq}. Our per-channel max-abs estimator is likewise standard. The question here is its behavioral importance when a quantized policy acts in an environment, not a new scalar quantizer or a claim that all fixed calibration schemes fail.

\paragraph{Precision allocation and behavioral proxies.}
Vision--language PTQ studies compensation, modality-aware channels, token saliency, and cross-layer dependencies~\cite{deng2026vlmptq,xue2026vlmq,wang2024qvlm,wang2026bivlm}. Mixed-precision allocation uses hardware feedback, Hessian sensitivity, reconstruction loss, or diffusion-specific proxies~\cite{wang2019haq,wang2019hawq,dong2020hawqv2,hubara2021accurate,li2021brecq,zhao2024mixdq}. We combine established geometry and distribution diagnostics~\cite{kornblith2019similarity,yin2026csaligner} with action-impact weights and byte costs to construct an initializer. Complete-policy scoring then audits proposals rather than treating additive layer benefits as sufficient. Our experiments distinguish this deviation-based decision rule from predicting task success: neither a rejected candidate nor a retained initializer establishes success optimality.
