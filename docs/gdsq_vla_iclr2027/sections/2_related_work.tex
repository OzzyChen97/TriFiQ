\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Efficient VLA inference.}
Generalist VLA policies combine large multimodal encoders with continuous-control heads~\cite{brohan2023rt2,octo2024,kim2024openvla,black2024pi0,intelligence2025pi05,bjorck2025groot}. Efficiency methods use compact architectures, token reduction, temporal reuse, or architecture--quantizer co-design~\cite{shukor2025smolvla,zhang2025molevla,yang2025efficientvla,xu2025vlacache,fang2025sqapvla}. These approaches may reduce computation as well as storage, but alter the network or its execution. \method instead preserves the pretrained checkpoint structure and studies static mixed-precision compression; we do not claim latency improvement for the eager fake-quant implementation.

\paragraph{VLA quantization.}
QuantVLA adapts distribution transformations to language and diffusion-action stacks~\cite{zhang2026quantvla}; ActQuant allocates tensor precision from action contribution and action-aware curvature~\cite{akbari2026actquant}; and $\Omega$-QVLA combines rotations with denoising-step scaling~\cite{wang2026omegaqvla}. DA-PTQ is closest to our control-level motivation because it uses trajectory motion drift for mixed-precision VLA quantization~\cite{xu2026daptq}. These methods improve how a VLA is scored or transformed on calibration data. Our focus is complementary: the compressed policy changes the future observations on which its own fidelity is evaluated. \dpac specifies physical-sequence deviation within this formulation, but the current experiments do not establish that it predicts closed-loop success better than DA-PTQ or simpler metrics.

\paragraph{VLM PTQ and mixed precision.}
Vision--language PTQ also exposes modality and cross-layer effects. VLM-PTQ uses residual-aware compensation and modality-aware channel treatment~\cite{deng2026vlmptq}; VLMQ weights reconstruction by token saliency~\cite{xue2026vlmq}; Q-VLM models cross-layer discretization dependencies~\cite{wang2024qvlm}; and Bi-VLM retains salient weights at higher precision in an ultra-low-bit design~\cite{wang2026bivlm}. These methods target vision--language understanding rather than continuous control, but they reinforce that independent layer scores can miss interactions. Our additive counterfactual program therefore generates candidates only; complete-network evaluation audits the finite proposals that it receives.

\paragraph{Transformer and diffusion PTQ.}
GPTQ and AWQ use second-order information or activation salience for weight-only quantization~\cite{frantar2022gptq,lin2024awq}. Weight--activation PTQ reshapes difficult distributions through smoothing, calibration, and rotations~\cite{xiao2023smoothquant,shao2024omniquant,ashkboos2024quarot,lin2024duquant,liu2025spinquant,sun2025flatquant}. Diffusion PTQ further addresses timestep-dependent ranges and iterative error accumulation~\cite{shang2023ptq4dm,li2023qdiffusion,wu2024ptq4dit,chen2024qdit,zhao2024mixdq}. These approaches generally calibrate on a fixed sample distribution, even when ranges vary across diffusion steps. \method builds on a Hessian-aware group-64 W4 substrate but asks a different question: how should PTQ be evaluated when quantization changes the policy-induced deployment distribution? Its deployment response removes the frozen A8 lookup; we do not claim a new weight-rounding primitive.

\paragraph{Mixed precision and behavioral sensitivity.}
Prior mixed-precision methods rank layers using hardware feedback, Hessian sensitivity, reconstruction loss, or diffusion-specific proxies~\cite{wang2019haq,wang2019hawq,dong2020hawqv2,hubara2021accurate,li2021brecq,zhao2024mixdq}. Representation similarities such as CKA and distribution divergences are useful diagnostics~\cite{kornblith2019similarity,yin2026csaligner}, but a local feature discrepancy does not identify how errors accumulate in executed control or whether interacting precision choices remain acceptable in the complete policy. We therefore separate proposal from acceptance: local evidence generates candidates, while an abstaining full-policy audit retains final authority under the deployment byte budget.
