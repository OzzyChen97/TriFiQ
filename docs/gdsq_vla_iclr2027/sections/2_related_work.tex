\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Efficient VLA inference.}
Generalist VLA policies combine large multimodal encoders with continuous-control heads~\cite{brohan2023rt2,octo2024,kim2024openvla,black2024pi0,intelligence2025pi05,bjorck2025groot}. Efficiency methods reduce their cost through compact architectures, dynamic depth, token reduction, or temporal reuse~\cite{shukor2025smolvla,zhang2025molevla,yang2025efficientvla,xu2025vlacache}. SQAP-VLA co-designs quantization-aware token pruning with the quantizer to obtain structured inference acceleration~\cite{fang2025sqapvla}. These directions can reduce computation as well as storage, but modify architecture, token flow, or execution. Our allocation leaves the checkpoint and operator graph intact; consequently, our present claim is static low-bit storage rather than latency acceleration.

\paragraph{VLA post-training quantization.}
QuantVLA adapts DuQuant-style transformations to language and diffusion-action stacks and calibrates attention temperature and output-head energy~\cite{zhang2026quantvla}. ActQuant is the closest action-guided mixed-precision method: it assigns tensor bit widths from action contribution and optimizes block scales with action-aware curvature, targeting sub-4-bit deployment~\cite{akbari2026actquant}. $\Omega$-QVLA instead pursues full-stack W4A4 quantization of both the language backbone and diffusion head using composite rotations and denoising-step scaling~\cite{wang2026omegaqvla}. These methods establish that control signals and the action head require special treatment. \method addresses a different question: which layers should retain FP16 under a fixed byte budget when local representation damage, downstream action sensitivity, and complete-mask interactions disagree. We preregister a pinned-code LIBERO reproduction and report only our locally audited values before making any shared-protocol ranking.

\paragraph{Transformer and diffusion PTQ.}
Weight-only methods such as GPTQ and AWQ optimize rounding or protect activation-salient channels~\cite{frantar2022gptq,lin2024awq}. Weight--activation PTQ reshapes difficult distributions through smoothing, calibration, or rotations~\cite{xiao2023smoothquant,shao2024omniquant,ashkboos2024quarot,lin2024duquant,liu2025spinquant,sun2025flatquant}. Diffusion PTQ additionally handles timestep-dependent activation ranges and iterative error accumulation~\cite{shang2023ptq4dm,li2023qdiffusion,wu2024ptq4dit,chen2024qdit,zhao2024mixdq}. These techniques improve the low-bit numerical substrate. We reuse a DuQuant-style W4A8 substrate so that the studied variable is precision allocation and correction selection, not a new quantizer kernel.

\paragraph{Mixed precision and representation evidence.}
Mixed-precision allocation has been driven by hardware feedback, Hessian sensitivity, reconstruction loss, and task-specific diffusion metrics~\cite{wang2019haq,wang2019hawq,dong2020hawqv2,hubara2021accurate,li2021brecq,zhao2024mixdq}. SVCCA and projection-weighted CCA compare representation subspaces~\cite{raghu2017svcca,morcos2018pwcca}, while CKA measures sample geometry with invariance to orthogonal transformations and isotropic scaling~\cite{kornblith2019similarity}. CS divergence provides a complementary distribution-overlap statistic~\cite{yin2026csaligner}. \method uses these metrics as frozen intervention probes, scales them with paired action sensitivity, and reserves action-functional evaluation for complete masks. Its runtime selector is separate from mask search: a preregistered model-level gate admits at most one calibrated correction and cannot use task identity or rollout outcomes.
