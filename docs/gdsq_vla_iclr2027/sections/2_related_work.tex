\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Efficient VLA inference.}
Generalist VLA policies combine large multimodal encoders with continuous-control heads~\cite{brohan2023rt2,octo2024,kim2024openvla,black2024pi0,intelligence2025pi05,bjorck2025groot}. Efficiency methods use compact architectures, token reduction, temporal reuse, or architecture--quantizer co-design~\cite{shukor2025smolvla,zhang2025molevla,yang2025efficientvla,xu2025vlacache,fang2025sqapvla}. These approaches may reduce computation as well as storage, but alter the network or its execution. \method instead preserves the pretrained checkpoint structure and studies static mixed-precision compression; we do not claim latency improvement for the eager fake-quant implementation.

\paragraph{VLA quantization.}
QuantVLA adapts distribution transformations to language and diffusion-action stacks~\cite{zhang2026quantvla}. ActQuant allocates tensor precision from action contribution and action-aware curvature~\cite{akbari2026actquant}, while $\Omega$-QVLA targets full-stack W4A4 with rotations and denoising-step scaling~\cite{wang2026omegaqvla}. These methods show that control heads and time-varying distributions require specialized treatment. Our distinction is the decision context: \method judges W4/FP16 changes through physical, prefix-accumulated action error in the complete policy, and deploys a single static mask without a correction selector.

\paragraph{Transformer and diffusion PTQ.}
GPTQ and AWQ use second-order information or activation salience for weight-only quantization~\cite{frantar2022gptq,lin2024awq}. Weight--activation PTQ reshapes difficult distributions through smoothing, calibration, and rotations~\cite{xiao2023smoothquant,shao2024omniquant,ashkboos2024quarot,lin2024duquant,liu2025spinquant,sun2025flatquant}. Diffusion PTQ further addresses timestep-dependent ranges and iterative error accumulation~\cite{shang2023ptq4dm,li2023qdiffusion,wu2024ptq4dit,chen2024qdit,zhao2024mixdq}. \method builds on a Hessian-aware group-64 W4 substrate. Its quantization novelty lies in full-context precision protection and dynamic per-forward activation ranges, not in a new weight-rounding primitive.

\paragraph{Mixed precision and behavioral sensitivity.}
Prior mixed-precision methods rank layers using hardware feedback, Hessian sensitivity, reconstruction loss, or diffusion-specific proxies~\cite{wang2019haq,wang2019hawq,dong2020hawqv2,hubara2021accurate,li2021brecq,zhao2024mixdq}. Representation similarities such as CKA and distribution divergences are useful diagnostics~\cite{kornblith2019similarity,yin2026csaligner}, but a local feature discrepancy does not identify how errors accumulate in executed control. \dpac instead operates on paired physical action sequences, and \fcp reserves final authority for complete-network, multi-context evaluation under the deployment byte budget.
