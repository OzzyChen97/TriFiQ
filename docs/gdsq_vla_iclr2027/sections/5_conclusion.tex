\section{Conclusion and Limitations}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presents \method, a training-free framework that couples action-weighted geometry/distribution preservation with byte-constrained W4/FP16 allocation and a calibration-gated selector over baseline, ATM, and OHB. The selector uses neither task identity nor outcome feedback, and its chosen paths are bitwise exact on 256 frozen observations per policy family.
