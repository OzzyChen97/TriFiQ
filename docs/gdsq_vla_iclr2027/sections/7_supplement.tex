\subsection{Action-Weighted CS--CKA Allocation Details}
\label{sec:init_provenance}

We construct the GR00T initializer before the full-context search. Its representation-stability score is $S_i=(\lambda_g\bar d_i^{\mathrm{geom}}+\lambda_d\bar d_i^{\mathrm{dist}})/(\lambda_g+\lambda_d)$, where $\bar d_i^{\mathrm{geom}}$ and $\bar d_i^{\mathrm{dist}}$ are jointly normalized versions of $1-\widehat{\mathrm{CKA}}_i$ and $\widehat D_{\mathrm{CS},i}$, respectively. Action-impact weights $w_i$ prioritize layers whose one-layer intervention changes the physical action output, while RMS and saturation guards retain unstable layers in FP16. A greedy byte-constrained solver followed by fixed-size layer flips produces nine complete candidate masks. Paired adjudication on 16 calibration observations selects one mask for each weighting without using success labels. Together, these steps form the guarded representation-stability initializer.

A four-task development sweep then selects $\lambda_g:\lambda_d=16:1$ by closed-loop macro success, yielding the 100-W4/16-FP16 mask used as $M_0$. CKA and CS statistics are established tools; the claimed design unit is their constrained conversion into a deployable precision mask followed by the full-context audit, not either statistic in isolation.

The full-context stage runs once from the frozen $M_0$; it does not restart from several initial masks and retain the best-performing one. The artifact registry records the exact initializer and calibration-buffer hash. The four ratio-selection tasks remain in the 50-task aggregate. A post-freeze calculation over the other 46 tasks gives 51.8\% for ours, 27.6\% for QuantVLA W4A8, and 52.8\% for FP16, showing that the four development tasks do not create the main pattern.

\subsection{Exact Metric Specification and Edge Cases}
\label{sec:metric_specification}

\paragraph{Sequence construction and action semantics.}
Each metric unit is a task--seed sequence $u$ with $R_u$ unique replans. We sort replans by their recorded integer index; each contributes the complete inverse-normalized $(16,12)$ chunk executed before the next replan, so $N_u=16R_u$. We discard later unexecuted forecasts, do not overlap adjacent chunks, and do not deduplicate predictions. A sequence contains only complete chunks collected before success, termination, truncation, or the official horizon. Duplicate replans, any shape other than $(16,12)$, or a non-finite value fail the metric gate rather than being padded or imputed. Coordinates 1--3 encode end-effector translation, 4--6 a rotation vector, 7 gripper close, and 8--12 the deployed base/control commands. Equation~\ref{eq:global_scale} normalizes each coordinate separately.

For coordinate $k$, we estimate a robust scale once from the complete FP16 selection buffer and never recompute it per task, sequence, or candidate:
\begin{equation}
 \tilde{s}_k=\max\!\left(1.4826\operatorname{MAD}_k,0.1\operatorname{RMS}_k\right),\qquad
 s_k=\max\!\left(\tilde{s}_k,0.05\operatorname{median}_{j}\tilde{s}_j,10^{-4}\right).
 \label{eq:global_scale}
\end{equation}
With $z_{u,t}=(a^Q_{u,t}-a^T_{u,t})/s$ and $\rho(z)=2(\sqrt{1+z^2}-1)$, the local and accumulated terms are
\begin{equation}
 d_{\mathrm{local}}^{(u)}=\frac{1}{N_ud}\sum_{t,k}\rho(z_{u,t,k}),\qquad
 d_{\mathrm{prefix}}^{(u)}=\frac{1}{N_ud}\sum_{t,k}
 \rho\!\left(\frac{\sum_{j=1}^{t}z_{u,j,k}}{\sqrt{t}}\right).
 \label{eq:local_prefix}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Pose map.}
For $\xi=[v;\omega]$, let $\Omega=[\omega]_{\times}$, $\theta=\lVert\omega\rVert_2$, and
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Exp}(\xi)
&=\begin{bmatrix}
I+A\Omega+B\Omega^2&(I+B\Omega+C\Omega^2)v\\
0&1
\end{bmatrix},\\
A&=\frac{\sin\theta}{\theta},\qquad
B=\frac{1-\cos\theta}{\theta^2},\qquad
C=\frac{\theta-\sin\theta}{\theta^3}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:se3_exp_detail}
\end{equation}
The implementation uses Taylor limits for $\theta<10^{-5}$. Right multiplication, $T_t=T_{t-1}\operatorname{Exp}(\xi_t)$, interprets each increment in the current end-effector frame. The logarithm uses the principal rotation angle and inverse left Jacobian $V^{-1}=I-\Omega/2+c\Omega^2$, where $c=1/12$ near zero and $c=\theta^{-2}-(1+\cos\theta)/(2\theta\sin\theta)$ otherwise. The three control-specific components are
\begin{align}
 e^{\mathrm{pose}}_t&=\frac{\operatorname{Diag}(s_{1:6})^{-1}
 \operatorname{Log}((T^T_t)^{-1}T^Q_t)}{\sqrt t},\qquad
 d_{\mathrm{pose}}^{(u)}=\overline\rho_t(e^{\mathrm{pose}}_t),
 \label{eq:pose}\\
 d_{\mathrm{stitch}}&=\overline{\rho}\!\left(
 \frac{(A^Q_{r+1,1}-A^Q_{r,H})-(A^T_{r+1,1}-A^T_{r,H})}{s}
 \right),
 \label{eq:stitch}\\
 d_{\mathrm{grip}}^{(u)}&=\tfrac12\left[
 \overline\rho_t(g^Q_t-g^T_t)+
 \overline\rho_{t>1}((g^Q_t-g^Q_{t-1})-(g^T_t-g^T_{t-1}))\right],
 \label{eq:gripper}
\end{align}
where $g^P_t=\sigma(4a^P_{t,7}/s_7)$. Translation and rotation are normalized coordinatewise before averaging.

\paragraph{Functional diagnostic.}
For physical final chunks indexed by context $i$, define
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 e_i&=\frac{\lVert A_i^Q-A_i^T\rVert_F^2}
 {\max(\lVert A_i^T\rVert_F^2,10^{-8})},\qquad
 d_{\mathrm{final}}=\frac{\lVert A^Q-A^T\rVert_F^2}
 {\max(\lVert A^T\rVert_F^2,10^{-8})},\\
 \dfunc&=d_{\mathrm{final}}+d_{\mathrm{kin}}+d_{\mathrm{grip}}
 +2\operatorname{CVaR}_{0.9}(d_{\mathrm{solver}}).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:dfunc_detail}
\end{equation}
$d_{\mathrm{kin}}$ is one half of the sum of analogous relative errors restricted to translation and rotation coordinates. We set $d_{\mathrm{solver}}=\operatorname{mean}_i e_i$, and $\operatorname{CVaR}_{0.9}$ averages $e_i$ at or above its empirical 90th percentile. The registered \dfunc gripper term is the fraction of adjacent solver states for which active gripper changes ($>10^{-4}$ in both policies) disagree in sign. Because the shared selection path records only final physical chunks and not the internal solver trajectory, this legacy \dfunc gripper term is zero on that path. Gripper deviation still enters \dpac through Equation~\ref{eq:gripper} and the explicit component guard.

\paragraph{Evaluation order.}
For each candidate, we (1) validate paired records, sort replans, and inverse-normalize final chunks; (2) compute the global 12-coordinate scale once from all FP16 selection chunks; (3) evaluate Equations~\ref{eq:local_prefix}, \ref{eq:pose}, \ref{eq:stitch}, and \ref{eq:gripper} for each task--seed sequence; (4) form the five-component mean and worst-10\% sequence mean in Equation~\ref{eq:dpac_sequence}; and (5) compute task-level candidate-minus-$M_0$ differences, the jackknife standard error in Equation~\ref{eq:task_jackknife}, $J$, and the pose/stitch/gripper upper bounds. We set $d_{\mathrm{stitch}}=0$ for a one-replan sequence and the gripper-difference term to zero for a one-action sequence. Acceptance requires strict $J_{\mathrm{final}}<0$, non-positive component upper bounds, and the exact byte constraint; equality fails closed.

For $n_g$ task scalars $x_t$ in cluster $g$, the implemented leave-one-task-out jackknife standard error is
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{SE}^{\mathrm{jack}}(\bar{x})=
 \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{t\in g}(x_t-\bar{x})^2}{n_g(n_g-1)}},
 \label{eq:task_jackknife}
\end{equation}
which is algebraically identical to the usual sample standard error of the task mean.

\subsection{Frozen GR00T Decision Record}

The GR00T search starts from frozen $M_0$. Corrected task-level statistics identify two of 116 coordinates with positive conservative benefit for retaining FP16 under both \dfunc and \dpac. Both are already protected: removing their protection increases the proxy loss, rather than providing two improving flips. The generator rebuilds six complete protected sets containing one to five FP16 layers, not small edits of the 16-layer anchor. All six are scored on teacher-state contexts over the three registered splits. Their objectives lie in $[0.948,2.360]$; each also fails at least one component guard. The protocol retains $M_0$ without candidate-state scoring. This decision neither exhausts the feasible neighborhood nor establishes superiority in task success; no prior rollout, byte count, or checkpoint is changed.

The final plan uses signed-nibble group-64 weights, identity row rotation, and per-forward dynamic A8, with no ATM, OHB, error folding, calibration bypass, or runtime selector. Its SHA-256 is recorded in the artifact registry and checked before table generation.

\subsection{Exact-Budget Offline Proxy Predictive Validity}
\label{sec:predictive_validity}

As an auxiliary diagnostic, we test whether frozen offline scores rank the closed-loop consequences of precision allocation. The library contains 60 masks with identical 100-W4/16-FP16 allocation and exact static bytes, stratified into ten masks at each of six swap distances from $M_0$. We freeze offline scores before running one rollout per target task and mask. Across all masks, Spearman correlation with signed success degradation is $-0.116$ for \dpac and $-0.028$ for MSE. Mask distance confounds this pooled comparison: larger swaps have greater offline distortion but, in this library, slightly higher mean success. These results position \dpac as a predeclared complete-policy deviation guard rather than a success-ranking objective.
