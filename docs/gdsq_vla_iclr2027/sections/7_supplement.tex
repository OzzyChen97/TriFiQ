\section{Supplementary Evidence}
\label{sec:supplement}

\subsection{Frozen GR00T Decision Record}

The GR00T search starts from an initialized 100-W4/16-FP16 mask. All 116 valid one-layer flips have non-positive benefit under the cross-context score. The exact dynamic program therefore emits no beneficial half- or full-budget combination, and five additional structured proposals are evaluated as complete networks alongside the context baseline. Every alternative has a positive paired minimax objective or violates pose, stitch, or gripper safety. The frozen fallback is the unchanged context baseline. These facts support local optimality only within the declared counterfactual neighborhood.

The final plan uses signed-nibble group-64 weights, identity row rotation, per-forward dynamic A8, and no ATM, OHB, error-folding, calibration bypass, or runtime selector. Its SHA-256 is recorded in the artifact registry and checked before table generation.

\subsection{Paired Statistical Detail}

Against QuantVLA W4A8, 1,647 of 2,500 episodes are tied, 721 favor ours, and 132 favor QuantVLA. Exact two-sided McNemar testing gives $p=6.30\times10^{-99}$; Holm adjustment over the registered comparison family gives $p=1.89\times10^{-98}$. The task-then-seed hierarchical bootstrap interval for the success-rate difference is $[0.1912,0.2800]$. Against FP16, the corresponding counts are 1,889 ties, 292 wins, and 319 losses, with $p=0.2929$ and interval $[-0.0388,0.0176]$.

\subsection{Storage Accounting}

The GR00T FP16 static-component scope is 2,139,537,408 bytes. The final plan occupies 962,068,480 bytes, including 327,352,320 fixed bytes and packed mixed-precision target components, for 2.2239$\times$ compression. This is 0.9982$\times$ the QuantVLA cell of 963,772,416 bytes and satisfies the preregistered 1.10$\times$ ceiling. The $\pi_{0.5}$ FP16 scope is 4,416,602,112 bytes; its 1,634,828,288-byte plan gives 2.7016$\times$ compression.

These values are static packed estimates. Activations, CUDA workspaces, simulator state, and eager fake-quant caches are outside the scope; therefore no runtime speed or peak-memory conclusion follows from the compression ratios.

\subsection{$\pi_{0.5}$ Claim Guard}

The $\pi_{0.5}$ combined screen uses two ten-seed waves over five tasks, for 100 paired episodes per configuration. The 80-W4 reference plan succeeds in 33 episodes and the 121-W4 candidate in 29; there are seven candidate wins and eleven losses. Since this screen neither advances to the 2,500-episode matrix nor passes a superiority gate, it supports only the observed storage point. The paper registry records the generic status \texttt{quick\_screen\_does\_not\_support\_success\_superiority}.
