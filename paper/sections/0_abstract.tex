\begin{abstract}
Low-bit vision-language-action (VLA) deployment differs from conventional model quantization: compression changes the policy, and the changed policy changes the observations it encounters. A precision mask that lowers action error on a fixed calibration buffer is therefore evaluated on observations the deployed policy may never see. We formulate deployment as \emph{byte-constrained policy configuration under a mask-induced rollout distribution}: choose the mask that maximizes task success under an exact storage budget, with the expectation taken over the rollout distribution the mask itself induces. The formulation separates two roles that tensor-level quantization merges. Representation-level evidence proposes where precision is valuable, while only complete-policy action evidence decides whether an assembled configuration is admissible. We instantiate this proposal--admission separation in \method. Representation-Integrity-aware Precision Allocation (RIPA) combines relational and distributional representation damage with action-impact weights to propose a guarded layout. Full-Context Precision Protection (FCP) projects that layout into a byte-feasible configuration and evaluates complete candidates through D-PAC, which defines temporal policy deviation through accumulated bias, replanning continuity, and control transitions; pointwise action error cannot distinguish these behaviors, so admission rests on sequence-level deviation. \dyrange then removes the remaining closed-loop source of fidelity loss: the activation-range shift induced by policy-changed inputs, handled by input-conditioned A8 ranges while precision stays frozen, with no gradient fine-tuning or runtime precision routing. Across 50 RoboCasa365 tasks and 2,500 episodes per headline configuration, \method attains the highest success among the evaluated PTQ methods on both backbones. GR00T N1.5 reaches \textbf{54.0\%} while reducing audited Linear-component storage by \textbf{2.22$\times$}, versus 30.4\% for approximately size-matched QuantVLA and 55.1\% for FP16. On $\pi_{0.5}$, it reaches \textbf{27.7\%} at \textbf{2.70$\times$} audited component compression, and the maximal-rate endpoint of the same rate family retains 26.8\% at 3.56$\times$. With the precision mask and quantized weights held fixed, input-conditioned A8 improves success by 10.6 percentage points over pooled static-A8 calibration. Artifacts are available at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/DyPAC-VLA}.
\end{abstract}
