\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Efficient VLA deployment.}
Generalist VLA policies combine multimodal encoders with continuous-control heads~\citep{brohan2023rt2,octo2024,kim2024openvla,black2024pi0,intelligence2025pi05,bjorck2025groot}. Compact architectures, token reduction, temporal reuse, and architecture--quantizer co-design reduce computation as well as storage~\citep{shukor2025smolvla,zhang2025molevla,yang2025efficientvla,xu2025vlacache,fang2025sqapvla}. We retain the pretrained architecture and study its success--storage tradeoff; Appendix~\ref{sec:hardware_results} reports the backend's memory benefit and latency cost.

\paragraph{Action-aware VLA quantization.}
QuantVLA adapts distribution transformations to language and diffusion-action stacks~\citep{zhang2026quantvla}; ActQuant allocates tensor precision using action contribution and action-aware curvature~\citep{akbari2026actquant}; $\Omega$-QVLA combines rotations with denoising-step scaling~\citep{wang2026omegaqvla}; and DA-PTQ uses trajectory motion drift for mixed-precision VLA quantization~\citep{xu2026daptq}. These methods share our premise that compression should be judged by its effect on control, and several already use action-side signals to allocate precision. The difference is where the decision is made. RIPA's representation-level allocation only proposes a layout; FCP projects it to an exact byte budget and admits a complete candidate solely on full-policy temporal evidence; input-conditioned execution handles the remaining activation shift. Local adaptations of ActQuant and DA-PTQ are separated from their source protocols.

\paragraph{Calibration and dynamic activation ranges.}
Weight--activation PTQ uses smoothing, calibration, or rotations~\citep{xiao2023smoothquant,shao2024omniquant,ashkboos2024quarot,lin2024duquant,liu2025spinquant,sun2025flatquant}, and diffusion PTQ addresses timestep-dependent ranges and iterative error accumulation~\citep{shang2023ptq4dm,li2023qdiffusion,wu2024ptq4dit,chen2024qdit,zhao2024mixdq}. Input-dependent scaling is established in ZeroQuant~\citep{yao2022zeroquant} and ViDiT-Q~\citep{zhao2024viditq}, and our per-channel max-abs estimator is likewise standard. We ask a behavioral question rather than a quantizer question: what happens to a frozen range table once the quantized policy changes the inputs it must cover.

\paragraph{Precision allocation and behavioral proxies.}
Vision--language PTQ studies compensation, modality-aware channels, token saliency, and cross-layer dependencies~\citep{deng2026vlmptq,xue2026vlmq,wang2024qvlm,wang2026bivlm}. Mixed-precision allocation uses hardware feedback, Hessian sensitivity, reconstruction loss, or diffusion-specific proxies~\citep{wang2019haq,wang2019hawq,dong2020hawqv2,hubara2021accurate,li2021brecq,zhao2024mixdq}. RIPA combines established relational and distributional statistics~\citep{kornblith2019similarity,yin2026csaligner} with action-impact weights, numerical guards, and byte costs, without assuming that additive layer costs capture all interactions or that reference fidelity guarantees success.

\paragraph{The decision-level gap: tensor optimization versus complete-policy configuration.}
The literature above optimizes a fidelity proxy---reconstruction error, Hessian sensitivity, salience, or per-tensor bit width---and then deploys the resulting tensor-level solution. That pipeline inherits two assumptions that a closed-loop policy violates: that proxy improvements compose when layers change together, and that fidelity to a reference under a fixed buffer predicts behavior under the distribution the quantized policy induces. Work on input-conditioned computation shows that deployment-time gating and reuse change what the model sees~\citep{xu2025vlacache,zhang2025molevla}, and that acceleration gates can reduce closed-loop reliability without a clear action-level signature. We treat tensor-level evidence as a proposal mechanism and place the decision at the level of the complete configuration, evaluated on paired action sequences against the anchor the budget can support.
